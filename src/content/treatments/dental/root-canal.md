## What is root canal treatment?

Root canal treatment, also called endodontic treatment, deals with infection inside a tooth. The soft tissue in the middle of the tooth, the pulp, can become infected or inflamed after deep decay or an injury. That can be very painful and can lead to an abscess.

During treatment we remove the infected pulp, clean and disinfect the inside of the tooth, then fill and seal it.

## What it is like

Root canal treatment has a worse reputation than it deserves. It is done under local anaesthetic, and most people compare it to having a filling, though the appointment is longer. We use rotary instruments and digital X-rays, which help keep the time in the chair down.
