## What is a dental emergency?

A dental emergency is a problem that needs attention quickly: to relieve severe pain, stop bleeding, save a damaged tooth or treat an infection. The usual causes are severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, an abscess, a lost filling or crown, and facial swelling.

If you are not sure whether your problem counts, call us and we will advise you.

## Emergency dental care at ProDenta

We keep appointments free each day for urgent cases. When you call, we tell you how to manage the problem until we can see you.

At the appointment we deal with the immediate problem first. If you need more treatment afterwards, we explain what it involves and what it costs before going any further.
