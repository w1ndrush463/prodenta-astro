## What is emergency dental care?

Dental emergencies are unexpected situations requiring immediate attention to relieve severe pain, stop bleeding, save a damaged tooth, or treat infection. Common emergencies include severe toothache, knocked-out teeth, broken teeth, dental abscesses, lost fillings or crowns, and facial swelling.

At ProDenta, we reserve same-day appointments for urgent cases and provide guidance on manageing your emergency until you can see us. Our experienced team is trained to handle all types of dental emergencies.

## Emergency dental care at ProDenta

At ProDenta, we understand that dental emergencies are stressful and often painful. That's why we prioritise urgent cases and ensure same-day appointments are available.
