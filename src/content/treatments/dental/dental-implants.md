## What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post that is placed in the jawbone, where it acts as an artificial tooth root. Once the bone has grown around it, a crown is attached to the top.

An implant does not rely on the teeth next to it for support, as a bridge does, and it does not move when you eat or speak. It also helps to prevent the bone loss that usually follows losing a tooth.
