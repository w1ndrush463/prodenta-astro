## What are fixed braces?

Fixed braces are brackets bonded to the front of each tooth and joined by a wire. The wire applies light, constant pressure that moves the teeth over time. They are used to straighten teeth and to correct the way the upper and lower teeth meet.

We fit two kinds. Metal braces are the hardest wearing. Ceramic braces have tooth-coloured brackets, so they are less noticeable.

## Choosing between metal and ceramic

Both types work in the same way and take about as long. The choice mostly comes down to how visible you want the braces to be and what you want to spend. We go through both options, with costs, at the free consultation.
