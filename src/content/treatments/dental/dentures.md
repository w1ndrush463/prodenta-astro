## What are dentures?

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth. A partial denture replaces a few teeth and fits around the ones you still have. A full (complete) denture replaces all the teeth in the upper or lower jaw.

Each denture is made from impressions of your mouth. The shade and shape of the teeth are chosen to suit your face and any remaining teeth.

## Dentures at ProDenta

Dentures are made by a dental technician from the impressions and measurements we take. The process takes 4 to 6 weeks and includes a try-in, where you can check the fit and appearance before the denture is finished.

New dentures take a few weeks to get used to. We show you how to wear and clean them, and we can adjust them if they rub or feel loose.
