## What happens at your first appointment?

The first appointment takes 45 to 60 minutes. We ask about your health and dental history, examine your teeth, gums and mouth, take X-rays if they are needed and carry out an oral cancer screening.

Afterwards we explain what we found and what, if anything, we recommend. You leave with a treatment plan and prices, and you decide what to do next.

## If you are nervous

Seeing a new dentist can be daunting, especially after a long gap. Tell us when you book or when you arrive. We treat anxious patients regularly, and we can slow down, explain each step and stop whenever you ask.
