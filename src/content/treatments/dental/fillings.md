## What is a dental filling?

A filling repairs a tooth that has been damaged by decay or a small fracture. Decay leaves a hole in the tooth. The hole has to be cleaned out and filled, or it gets bigger and can lead to pain and infection.

There are two main types. Composite (white) fillings are tooth-coloured and bond directly to the tooth. Amalgam (silver) fillings are the traditional kind and are more durable in large cavities in back teeth.

## Fillings at ProDenta

We remove only the decayed part of the tooth and keep as much healthy tooth as we can.

Most fillings are done under local anaesthetic in one appointment. The numbness wears off in 2 to 3 hours.
