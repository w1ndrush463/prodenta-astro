## What is orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontics is the branch of dentistry that moves teeth. It is used to straighten crooked or crowded teeth, close gaps and correct the way the upper and lower teeth meet.

There are several ways to do it. Fixed braces are attached to the teeth for the whole of treatment. Clear aligners such as Invisalign are removable. Which suits you depends on what needs correcting, how visible you want the brace to be and your budget.

## Starting treatment

It starts with a free consultation. We examine your teeth, explain which options would work for you and give you a quote for each. Treatment takes between 6 and 24 months, with a check-up every 4 to 8 weeks.
