## What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth with a series of clear plastic aligners. There are no metal brackets or wires, and you take the aligners out to eat and to clean your teeth.

Each set of aligners is made for your teeth and worn for about two weeks before you move on to the next. Each set moves your teeth a little further towards the planned position.

## Invisalign at ProDenta

We plan your treatment from digital scans, so you can see how your teeth are expected to move before you decide. After that we see you every 6 to 8 weeks to check progress.

When your teeth have reached their planned position, you wear retainers to keep them there.
