## What is the Perfect Peel?

The Perfect Peel is a medical-grade chemical peel featuring a unique blend of powerful ingredients including TCA, retinoic acid, phenol, salicylic acid, vitamin C, and glutathione. This comprehensive formula treats multiple skin concerns simultaneously, delivering transformative results with minimal downtime.

At ProDenta, we use The Perfect Peel to address pigmentation, acne scarring, fine lines, sun damage, and uneven skin tone. Suitable for all skin types and tones, this treatment provides dramatic rejuvenation while remaining gentle enough to avoid extended recovery periods.
