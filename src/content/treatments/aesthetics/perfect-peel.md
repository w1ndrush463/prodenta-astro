## What is the Perfect Peel?

The Perfect Peel is a medical-grade chemical peel. It contains TCA, retinoic acid, phenol, salicylic acid, vitamin C and glutathione.

We use it for pigmentation, sun damage, acne scarring, fine lines and uneven tone. Expect about a week of visible peeling.

## The Perfect Peel at ProDenta

The peel is applied by practitioners trained in its use. We see you for a consultation first, and explain how to prepare and how to look after your skin while it peels.
