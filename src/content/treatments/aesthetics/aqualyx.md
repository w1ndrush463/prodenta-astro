## What is Aqualyx?

Aqualyx is an injectable treatment for small areas of fat that have not shifted with diet and exercise. The solution breaks down fat cells, and your body clears them over the following weeks.

We use it most often under the chin and along the jawline. It is a non-surgical treatment, so the change comes gradually over a course of sessions.

## Aqualyx at ProDenta

We use genuine Aqualyx and see you for a consultation before any treatment. If Aqualyx is unlikely to give the result you want, we will say so.
