## What is wrinkle reduction?

Wrinkle reduction treats the lines that form when you make facial expressions, such as forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet. It is non-surgical.

It starts with a consultation, where we assess the lines and explain which options are suitable for you. The effect is temporary and usually lasts 3 to 4 months.

## Wrinkle reduction at ProDenta

We take a conservative approach and aim for a subtle result that keeps your natural expressions. Treatment is planned around your face and what you want to change.
