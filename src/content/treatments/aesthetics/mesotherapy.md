## What is mesotherapy?

Mesotherapy delivers vitamins, minerals, amino acids and antioxidants into the middle layer of the skin through a series of very small injections. We use it for dull or dehydrated skin, fine lines and uneven tone.

The improvement is gradual and builds over the course.

## Mesotherapy at ProDenta

We choose the formulation after looking at your skin and asking what you want to change. We use medical-grade ingredients and sterile technique.
