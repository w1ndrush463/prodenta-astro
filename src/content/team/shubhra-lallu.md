Shubhra completed her dental degree at the University of Melbourne in 2001, followed by a hospital officer post at Monash Medical Centre, where she trained alongside specialists in prosthodontics, endodontics and children's dentistry.

She moved to the UK in 2006 and has worked in private practice throughout. She treats children and adults, with a focus on preventive care, and has a particular interest in prosthodontics, restorative and cosmetic dentistry.
