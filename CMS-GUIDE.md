# Editing the ProDenta website

The website is edited with **Pages CMS**, a simple editor that runs in your browser. There is nothing to install.

## Logging in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** and sign in (your web designer will send you an invitation by email the first time).
2. Choose the **prodenta-astro** website.

## What you can change

The menu on the left has four areas.

### Pages
- **Home page**: the heading, photos, welcome text, the featured new-patient offer and the "Why ProDenta" points.
- **About page**, **Contact page**, **Team page (heading)**: headings, introductions and photos.
- **Fees page**: current offers, membership plans and every fee list. Open a fee list, then a group (for example "Repair"), to change a price or add a row.
- **Reviews page**: add, edit or remove patient reviews. Tick "Show this one on the home page" to feature a review.
- **Policy pages**: privacy policy, complaints and any other policy you add.

### Treatments
- **Dental treatments** and **Aesthetic treatments**: one entry per treatment. Each has a name, a one-line summary, a photo, the price, the main description, key facts, steps, benefits and questions and answers.
- Click **Add an entry** to create a new treatment. It appears in the menus and treatment lists automatically, under the category you choose.
- **Results gallery** is for genuine before-and-after photos of your own patients, with their written consent. The section stays hidden until you add photos.
- **Category names**: rename the groups (Maintain, Enhance and so on) or change their order.

### Team
One entry per person: name, role, group, photo, areas of interest and biography. If someone has no photo yet, their initials are shown instead.

### Site settings
- **Contact details and opening hours**: phone, WhatsApp, email, address, opening hours, social media links and the booking banner shown near the bottom of most pages. Change these once and they update everywhere.
- **Menus**: the links in the main menu and footer.

## Saving and going live

Click **Save** when you have finished editing an entry. The website rebuilds itself and your change is live in about two minutes.

## Photos

- Use the photo field to upload from your computer or choose a photo already on the site.
- Photos are resized automatically, but please keep uploads under 2MB. JPG is best for photographs.
- Always fill in the **Image description**. It is read aloud to people who use screen readers.

## Good to know

- **Opening hours** should be written like `8am to 6pm` (or `Closed`) so search engines can read them.
- Links to pages on this site start with a slash, for example `/contact` or `/fees`.
- The "Search engine listing (advanced)" section on each page is optional. If you leave it blank the page heading is used.
- The layout, colours and fonts are not editable here, so you cannot break the design. If you need a new type of section, ask your web designer.
